ABOUT

I build locomotion systems from dynamics to deployment.

Taehyun Kim
Ph.D. Student, Department of Electrical Engineering, Korea University
Control & Mechatronics Lab · KIST Humanoid Lab

My research focuses on dynamic locomotion and control for humanoid and wheel-legged robots. I am interested in combining interpretable model-based control with numerical optimization and reinforcement learning.

The common thread is physically meaningful decision-making under contact: footstep planning, contact-force optimization, whole-body coordination, predictive control, and learning-based adaptation.
