About

Robotics research
from models to hardware.

I am interested in building locomotion systems where model-based control, numerical optimization, and learning complement each other.

Research focus

My work spans humanoid and wheel-legged robots, with emphasis on dynamic contact transitions, whole-body optimization, predictive control, and learning-based adaptation. I aim to develop methods that remain physically interpretable while scaling to complex real-world locomotion tasks.
